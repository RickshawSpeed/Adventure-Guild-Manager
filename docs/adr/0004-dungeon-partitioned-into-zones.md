# A Dungeon is partitioned into independently simulated Zones

All Guilds share a Dungeon, but it is divided into Zones, each simulated independently; Parties only encounter others in the same Zone. A single undivided world makes encounters either overwhelming or meaningless at scale, and cannot be split across machines. Zones are the unit of horizontal scaling. Separate Realms (isolated copies of the game per population) were rejected; at higher player counts several Dungeons are open at once instead, and any Guild may send Expeditions into any open Dungeon.
